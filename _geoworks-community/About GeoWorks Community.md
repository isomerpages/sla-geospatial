<p>GeoWorks Community brings together working professionals and passionate
individuals across the geospatial landscape. Our aim is to create a vibrant
community where innovation thrives through meaningful connections. Through
events and knowledge-sharing sessions, we hope to create opportunities
for potential collaborations and knowledge exchange within the community.</p>
<p>For collaboration opportunities, please contact our team at <a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Geoworks@sla.gov.sg</a>
</p>
